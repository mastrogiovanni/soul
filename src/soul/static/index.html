Prepare a model

Download the model shards that fit on your nodes now. If more capacity is needed, the model shows a warning and distribution resumes automatically when nodes join.

Supports Llama, Mistral, Qwen2 and Qwen3 dense safetensors: unquantized, AWQ GEMM 4-bit or GPTQ v1 4-bit. Quantized models require updated nodes. Gated models require HF_TOKEN on the controller and nodes. Browse models and read the setup guide ↗
